## 2. Purpose

The review will give an independent, evidence-based judgement on:

1. What the strategy delivered against its KPIs and budget.
2. Which actions produced outcomes and impact, and which did not.
3. Value for money of the main investments (compute, data, talent,
   regulation, adoption).
4. What the next strategy should keep, change, stop and start.

## 4. Review questions

| # | Question | Main evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Were KPI targets met at input, output, outcome and impact level? | KPI reports, verified source data |
| Q2 | Was the budget released and spent as planned, by pillar and source? | Budget execution reports, audits |
| Q3 | Is shared compute used, by whom, at what cost per useful GPU-hour? | Utilisation logs, invoices |
| Q4 | Which public AI systems are live, and what did they change for citizens? | AI register, service data, user survey |
| Q5 | Did any system cause harm? How were complaints handled? | Complaints, AIA register, ombudsman |
| Q6 | Has talent grown and stayed? | Graduate tracking, vacancy data, retention |
| Q7 | Did regulation build trust without blocking useful services? | Regulator records, firm survey, trust survey |
| Q8 | Did the delivery unit and coordination routines work? | Decisions log, interviews |
| Q9 | How do results compare with two or three peer countries? | Published peer reviews and indices |
| Q10 | What should the next strategy prioritise, and what will it cost? | All of the above |

## 5. Method

The reviewers will propose a method. It should include at least:

- [ ] Document and data review, including verification of a sample
      of reported KPI values against source records.
- [ ] Interviews with at least ____ people across government,
      regulators, industry, academia and civil society.
- [ ] At least ____ case studies of individual use cases, including
      at least one that failed or was stopped.
- [ ] Analysis of cost per outcome for the main investments.
- [ ] A citizen or user survey, or analysis of an existing one.
- [ ] A validation workshop on draft findings.

Where data are missing, the report must say so plainly. Missing data
is a finding, not a gap to be filled with estimates.

## 6. Independence

6.1 Reviewers must not have delivered, advised on or supplied any
    strategy action in the review period, or must declare it.
6.2 The commissioning body may comment on factual accuracy only.
    The reviewers' judgements are their own.
6.3 The final report will be published in full within ____ days of
    receipt.
