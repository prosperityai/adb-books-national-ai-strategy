# AI Delivery Unit Charter

Country: ____________  Approved by: ____________  Date: ____________
Legal instrument: [ ] Cabinet decision  [ ] Decree  [ ] Act, section ____

## 1. Purpose

The AI Delivery Unit makes sure the National AI Strategy is delivered
on time and on budget, and reports honestly when it is not.

It does not own every AI project. Ministries own their projects; the
unit tracks them, removes blockages and escalates.

## 2. Mandate

The unit shall:

1. Maintain the strategy delivery plan, with milestones and owners.
2. Track every funded action and KPI, using data it can verify.
3. Run the stocktake routine in section 6.
4. Escalate blocked actions to the strategy owner within ____ days.
5. Keep the public register of government AI systems.
6. Manage the use-case portfolio and its gates with the finance
   ministry.
7. Publish the annual progress report.

The unit shall not:

- Buy or run AI systems for ministries (except shared services
  assigned to it by cabinet: ______________________).
- Regulate. Regulation stays with ______________________.
